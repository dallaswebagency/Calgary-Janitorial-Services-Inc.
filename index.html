About CJS

One crew matched to your building

Calgary Janitorial Services Inc. (CJS) has been serving Calgary area clients since 1964. We provide licensed, insured and bonded cleaning to commercial buildings on a daily, weekly or monthly basis to meet your company's unique needs.

We match the right crew to each job, and our managers keep checking the work so the standard stays high. Detail cleaning, individually customized for every client.
